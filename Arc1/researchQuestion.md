### Research topic and motivation

Modernization of legacy systems.

Legacy systems are extremely common in the industry and they result in many consequences. We would therefore like to investigate how modernize such a system in a good manner to facilitate continued evolution of the system. There are great risks when modernizing a legacy system, and without proper processes and practices to mitigate those risks the modernization process might fail.
